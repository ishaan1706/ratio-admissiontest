# ESAT 10-Week Study Plan

Assumes about 8 to 10 hours a week and three required modules (Mathematics 1 plus two others). Adjust the module weeks to your own choices. Original material; not official UAT-UK guidance.

| Week | Focus | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set-up and baseline | Read the official ESAT overview and content specification. Confirm your modules with the official course list. Sit Mock A for Mathematics 1 untimed to find gaps. Start the error log. |
| 2 | Mathematics 1 core | M1–M4 from the checklist. Learn the Mathematics 1 formula sheet. Flashcards daily (10 minutes). |
| 3 | Mathematics 1 completion | M5–M7. Sit Mathematics 1 Mock A timed (40 minutes). Log and revise every error. |
| 4 | Second module, first half | Work through the official guide for your second module. Flashcards. Formula sheet. |
| 5 | Second module, second half | Finish the checklist. Sit the second module's Mock A timed. Error log review. |
| 6 | Third module, first half | Official guide and checklist for your third module. Flashcards. |
| 7 | Third module, second half | Finish the checklist. Sit the third module's Mock A timed. |
| 8 | Mock B round | Sit Mock B for each of your modules, timed, on separate days. Re-revise the topics with the most errors. |
| 9 | Official past papers | ENGAA / NSAA Section 1 questions on your modules (skip those marked outside the ESAT specification), timed in blocks of 27 questions. Try the official Pearson VUE on-screen practice tests. |
| 10 | Final week | One full timed set of your modules back to back. Light revision of formula sheets and flashcards. Read the official test-day information and check your ID and booking. |

## Daily habits

- 10 minutes of flashcards for each module you are taking.
- 5 to 10 mental arithmetic questions without a calculator.
- Record every wrong or guessed answer in the error log on the same day.

## Mock routine

1. 40 minutes, no calculator, no notes, scrap paper only.
2. Answer every question (no negative marking).
3. Mark immediately. For every wrong or guessed answer, log the topic code, why it went wrong and the fix.
4. Re-attempt the wrong questions two days later without looking at the solution.

## Error log columns

See esat-error-log-template.csv: date, module, mock or source, question, topic code, error type, what went wrong, fix or rule to remember, re-attempt date, re-attempt result.

Error types: concept gap, misread question, arithmetic slip, unit or conversion, time pressure, guessed.
