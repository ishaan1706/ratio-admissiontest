# SAT Math formula reference

Open the live [Bluebook directions and reference sheet](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/english-sat-test-directions-bb.pdf) to check what is supplied. This is a Ratio-written study reference, not a copy of the official page.

## Supplied on the Math reference sheet

| Topic | What you can look up |
|---|---|
| Basic area and circumference | Rectangle lw; triangle bh/2; circle πr² and circumference 2πr. |
| Right triangles | a² + b² = c²; 45–45–90 side ratios 1:1:√2; 30–60–90 side ratios 1:√3:2. |
| Volume | Rectangular prism lwh; cylinder πr²h; sphere 4πr³/3; cone πr²h/3; rectangular pyramid lwh/3. |
| Angle facts | 360° or 2π radians in a circle; 180° for the interior angles of a triangle. |

## Know or derive; not on that reference sheet

| If you see… | Use… |
|---|---|
| Coordinates on a line | Slope (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁), if x₂ ≠ x₁; point-slope y − y₁ = m(x − x₁); intercept form y = mx + b when not vertical. |
| Distance or midpoint | Distance √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²); midpoint ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2). |
| Quadratic | a(x − h)² + k has vertex (h, k); for ax² + bx + c, vertex x = −b/(2a); roots (−b ± √(b² − 4ac))/(2a), a ≠ 0. |
| Factor or expand | a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b); (a ± b)² = a² ± 2ab + b². |
| Circle equation or part of a circle | (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²; arc length = (θ/360°)(2πr), sector area = (θ/360°)πr². For θ in radians: arc = rθ, sector = r²θ/2. |
| Right triangle angle | sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse; cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse; tan θ = opposite/adjacent. Complementary angles swap sine and cosine. |
| Exponential change | Start × (1 ± rate)^periods. Make the period agree with the stated growth interval. |
| Percent change | (new − old)/old × 100%; reverse a percent change by dividing by its multiplier. |
| Average, spread or residual | Mean = sum/count; weighted mean = Σ(value × count)/Σcount; range = max − min; residual = observed − predicted. |
| Probability | Favorable/equally likely possible; with a condition, restrict both counts to outcomes that satisfy it. |
| Rescaling a shape | With length factor k, areas scale by k², volumes by k³. |

**Example:** A circle with center (2, −3) and radius 8 has equation (x − 2)² + (y + 3)² = 64. A central angle of 90° selects a quarter of the circumference, giving arc length 4π, and a quarter of the area, giving sector area 16π.

**SPR:** Enter one answer; no units, commas, percent signs or mixed numbers. The official directions linked above give the five/six-character limits and rounding/truncation examples. All Ratio bank SPR items have a fitting exact entry.
